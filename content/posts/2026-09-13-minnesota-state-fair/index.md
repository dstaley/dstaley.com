## Vietnamese Iced Coffee

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="01-vietnamese-iced-coffee.jpeg" alt="Light brown iced coffee in a clear plastic cup with the Minnesota State Fair wordmark on it"> %}

This iced coffee is seriously one of the best coffee drinks I've ever had (right up there with the iced latte I had from [Duluth Coffee Company](/posts/one-year-in-minneapolis/#10-the-iced-vanilla-latte-i-got-from-duluth-coffee-company)). I had three this year (and would have gotten a fourth had I not forced myself to try the Campfire Cold Brew from Blue Barn). I'm trying to recreate this at home but haven't yet had much success.

From [Que Viet](https://www.instagram.com/quevietconcessions/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Amish Doughnut

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="02-amish-doughnut.jpeg" alt="Large glazed ring doughnut with a bite taken out"> %}

This was one of the items I missed out on last year due to the long lines. This year, Peachey's upgraded their operations to focus on getting through the line faster. Even though the line appeared long, I was in and out in under ten minutes. It reminded me a lot of a fresh, warm Krispy Kreme glazed doughnut. I do wish it was a bit thicker and more robust though, as it was a little difficult to eat given how floppy it was.

From [Peachey’s Baking Co.](https://www.instagram.com/peacheysbakingco/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Caramel Apple Iced Tea

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="03-caramel-apple-iced-tea.jpeg" alt="Amber iced tea topped with a thick layer of pale foam"> %}

This was a new item this year and from a vendor that I loved the tea from last year. As someone who grew up in the South, I'm intimately acquainted with iced tea. This caramel apple iced tea was made with an apple-infused tea and then topped with a caramel cold foam. The first few sips were just pure cold foam (still delicious), but after a few minutes the foam mixed with the tea, perfectly marrying the two flavors.

From [Loon Lake Iced Tea](https://www.instagram.com/loonlakeicedtea/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Pickle Pizza

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="04-pickle-pizza.jpeg" alt="A slice of pizza covered with melted cheese and pickle chips"> %}

This is a Minnesota State Fair staple, and I fully understand why. It ended up being my favorite item of the fair this year. I'm a huge fan of pickle on pizza, but this managed to perfectly balance the pickle with the dill seasoning and possibly a ranch-infused cheese of some kind? Majestic. 10/10. Would eat every week if I could.

From [Rick’s Pizza](https://www.instagram.com/rickspizza_/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Fried Cheese Curds

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="05-fried-cheese-curds.jpeg" alt="Fried cheese curds piled in a tray"> %}

It's fried cheese curds. There's really not much to say here.

From The Mouth Trap.

{% </two_up> %}

## Citrus Hibiscus Wine Slushie

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="06-citrus-hibicus-wine-slushie.jpeg" alt="Bright orange frozen drink in a clear plastic cup"> %}

This year I wanted to try at least one of the alcoholic beverages available, and a wine slushie sounded like exactly the right thing to go with. I'm not a huge fan of drinks that have high ABV, so this one at 5.3% ABV was perfect. This was also new this year, and I expect it'll be back again given how many people I saw trying it.

From Minnesota Wine Country.

{% </two_up> %}

## Grilled Peach with Goat Butter

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="07-grilled-peach-with-goat-butter.jpeg" alt="Two browned peach halves with a small mound of goat butter in the middle"> %}

This was a returning item from my visit last year. While it didn't hit the same, it was still an enjoyable contrast to all the other fried foods I was having.

From The Produce Exchange.

{% </two_up> %}

## Chicken and Chorizo Paella

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="08-chicken-and-chorizo-paella.jpeg" alt="Orange rice topped with a fried egg and green pepper"> %}

Once again, a returning favorite. This reminds me of jambalaya, and thus of home back in Louisiana. The fried egg is a must!

From [Paella Depot](https://www.instagram.com/paelladepot/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Prickly Pear and Orange Agua Fresca

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="09-prickly-pear-and-orange-agua-fresca.jpeg" alt="Pink iced drink with an orange wedge in a clear cup with the Minnesota State Fair wordmark"> %}

Last year I got the mango pineapple agua fresca, so I wanted to try something new this year. While this flavor wasn't my favorite, it was still super refreshing!

From [Paella Depot](https://www.instagram.com/paelladepot/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Campfire Cold Brew

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="10-campfire-cold-brew.jpeg" alt="Pale iced coffee topped with a small marshmallow"> %}

This is what I started my second day at the fair with. The additional flavors of marshmallow, chocolate, and graham cracker were subtle but present. The little marshmallow they added wasn't the freshest after sitting in the cold drink for a few minutes, but was still a nice touch.

From [Blue Barn](https://www.instagram.com/bluebarnmn/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Bacon, Egg, and Cheese Crêpe

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="11-bacon-egg-and-cheese-crepe.jpeg" alt="Folded crêpe filled with scrambled egg, cheese, and bacon pieces, wrapped in aluminum foil"> %}

Probably my least favorite thing at the fair this year (which explains why there were no lines at their booth). Totally fine, just a bit bland and unwieldy.

From French Crêperie.

{% </two_up> %}

## Chocolate Croissant

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="12-chocolate-croissant.jpeg" alt="Square flaky pastry dusted generously with powdered sugar"> %}

Since the crêpe was a bit disappointing, I decided to follow it up with this chocolate croissant from French Meadow right next door. It was fresh and flaky and had the perfect amount of chocolate in it.

From [French Meadow](https://www.instagram.com/frenchmeadowcafe/).

{% </two_up> %}

## French Fries

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="13-french-fries.jpeg" alt="Cup overflowing with thick, golden french fries"> %}

These are another State Fair staple, with two locations on the fairgrounds. I saw people walking around with giant buckets full of these french fries. There was apparently [a bit of drama](https://bringmethenews.com/minnesota-lifestyle/this-years-fresh-french-fries-drama-at-the-minnesota-state-fair-explained) where the vendor sourced their potatoes from another region resulting in a noticeable difference. People take their fries seriously! I thought they were perfectly adequate, but they didn't blow me out of the water.

From [Fresh French Fries](https://www.instagram.com/freshfrenchfries/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Frozen Grapes

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="14-frozen-grapes.jpeg" alt="Cup with purple grapes coated in frost"> %}

These were another returning favorite from last year. I don't usually eat grapes (mostly because I have two dogs at home who are always hovering around me when I'm eating something), so it was a nice palate cleanser after the french fries.

From Bayou Bob’s Gator Shack.

{% </two_up> %}

## Root Beer Float

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="15-root-beer-float.jpeg" alt="Foamy brown soda with scoops of vanilla ice cream in a clear cup"> %}

I'm a huge fan of root beer, and 1919 Root Beer is made in Minnesota! I was glad they had a small version of their root beer float since I didn't want a giant serving.

From 1919 Root Beer.

{% </two_up> %}

## Watermelon Mint Limeade

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="16-watermelon-mint-limeade.jpeg" alt="Bright red iced drink in a clear plastic cup"> %}

This was the standout drink for me at this year's fair. It perfectly balanced sweet with tart and helped support local farmers! Will definitely be getting it again next year.

From [Minnesota Farmers Union](https://www.instagram.com/mnfarmersunion/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Dill Pickle Iced Tea

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="17-dill-pickle-iced-tea.jpeg" alt="Amber iced tea with a seasoned rim and pickle spear"> %}

On day two I had to go back to my friends at Loon Lake Iced Tea for their dill pickle iced tea, which comes with a chamoy rim and a pickle spear.

From [Loon Lake Iced Tea](https://www.instagram.com/loonlakeicedtea/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Chicken Tandoori Quesaratha

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="18-chicken-tandoori-quesaratha.jpeg" alt="Two browned triangular stuffed flatbreads with dipping sauce"> %}

During my first year at the fair I completely missed the International Bazaar, so I didn't want to make the same mistake again this year. Holy Land has a pretty big presence there, but this specific item didn't really hit the spot for me.

From [Holy Land](https://www.instagram.com/holylandmn/).

{% </two_up> %}

## Red Velvet Fried Oreos

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="19-red-velvet-fried-oreos.jpeg" alt="Red fried Oreos drizzled with white icing"> %}

Last year, as I passed the Deep Fried Candy Bars booth, the worker would not let me walk by without trying one of their deep-fried red velvet Oreos. This year, instead of going for the original, I decided to get a full set of the red velvet version. Delightful.

From Deep Fried Candy Bars.

{% </two_up> %}

## Blueberry Basil Lemonade

{% <two_up base_path={page.colocated_path} src="20-blueberry-basil-lemonade.jpeg" alt="Deep pink iced drink in a clear plastic cup"> %}

As I walked out of the 2026 Minnesota State Fair, this is what I decided to grab. While I was hoping for a repeat of the Watermelon Mint Limeade, this one didn't strike the same balance, leaning more towards the tart side. Still good though!

From [Blue Barn](https://www.instagram.com/bluebarnmn/).

{% </two_up> %}
