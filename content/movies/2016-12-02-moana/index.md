I did not know it at the time, but this would become the first of many movies I saw at Queensgate Cinemas 12. I was visiting some friends unaware that seventh months later I'd be accepting a job and moving to the Tri-Cities. I also had my very first edible before seeing it, which is an experience I highly recommend (especially for "Shiny"). A little over a year later I'd be back to Queensgate Cinemas 12 to see [Star Wars: The Last Jedi](/movies/2017-12-16-star-wars-the-last-jedi) as a resident of the Tri-Cities.
