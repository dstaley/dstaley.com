I never got to see _La La Land_ when it was originally in theaters, so when they announced a limited run for the tenth anniversary in Dolby Cinema I jumped at the opportunity. Initially I was disappointed because even though I tried to buy tickets just three hours after the announcement, they were all sold out at my local theather. Thankfully, the next day they added additional showtimes. Seeing in a nice theater a film that I fall in love with more and more each time I watch it was such a delightful experience.
