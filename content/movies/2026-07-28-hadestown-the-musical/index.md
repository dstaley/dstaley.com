I had never listened to _Hadestown_, so having the chance to listen for the first time via a pro-shot was a chance I didn't want to pass up. I found the staging absolutely delightful, and really enjoyed Eva Noblezada's Eurydice. I wasn't the biggest fan of Reeve Carney's Orpheus, but that's less on his individual performance and more on how the role is written. After watching the pro-shot, I learned there's a fierce online battle between fans on their favorite Orpheus, and apparently Carney's portrayal is quite polarizing.
