I'm a big fan of movies that are in and of themselves events. Given the [history](https://deadline.com/2023/11/coyote-vs-acme-shelved-warner-bros-discovery-writeoff-david-zaslav-1235598676/) surrounding the film, when it finally was announced that it was coming to theaters I knew it was something I wanted to see. While the movie itself was a perfectly fine film, I think it's background made it even more enjoyable than the film itelf. John Cena's performance was incredible, and the "predator spray" line got a tremendous laugh out of the audience that it made me glad I got to see it with a bunch of people who at least understood the Loony Toons enough to get the joke. The ending was an unexpected punch to the gut in the best way.
