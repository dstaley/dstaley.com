I saw _Wicked_ during an initial trip to Minneapolis that I refer to as my "vibe check" trip. I wanted to see what the city was like, what it would be like to drive around and do stuff, and it just so happened to correspond with opening weekend. I saw the film in Dolby Cinema, which would quickly become my preferred way of watching movies at AMC Southdale 16.
